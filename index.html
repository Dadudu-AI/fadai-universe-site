01  /  Concept

Three seconds of nothing
is all it takes.

Daydreaming is not a break from listening — it is the deepest part of it. Fadai Universe gives that moment a body: your music, rendered as something alive, breathing on the screen in front of you.

  1. i

    You press play

    Any bound player — NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, Spotify, Apple Music. No setup, no account, no plugins.

  2. ii

    A small orb appears

    It floats in the corner of your desktop, swelling softly with the low end of whatever is playing. You can drag it anywhere.

  3. iii

    Then it takes the screen

    Music playing, computer idle for a minute — fullscreen, unprompted. Sound becomes light, and light becomes something that swims.

04  /  Details

Quiet in the
right places.

No tray icon. No notifications. No "sign in to continue". It watches one thing — whether a player you chose is running — and otherwise stays out of the way.

Bind once
Choose one or several players on first launch. The orb appears only when one of them is open; right-click it to re-bind.
Idle = invitation
Music playing and 60 seconds of no input → fullscreen. If it is already open in a window, it promotes itself.
Leaving is deliberate
Moving the mouse will not throw you out. Exit with the ⛶ button or ESC — no accidental interruptions.
Microphone, if you allow it
Ambient sound is analysed on-device for real-time spectrum. Nothing is recorded, nothing leaves the machine.
Built on Electron + Three.js
Particle shaders, bloom post-processing, and a native Swift probe for player detection on macOS.
